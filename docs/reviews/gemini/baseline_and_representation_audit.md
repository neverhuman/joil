## 1. Independent Diagnosis of `ct35-pilot-tet1` Low-Determinant Cells (Inlet Tangency Cusp)

### 1.1 Exact Coordinate Verification
An independent inspection of `runs/ct35-pilot-tet1` (`391,062` tetrahedra generated by `physics/openfoam/mesh/gmsh_mesh.py`) confirms Codex's coordinate diagnosis:
- All topological, volume, skewness (`max 2.138`), non-orthogonality (`max 62.66°`, `avg 15.86°`), and boundary-patch area/centroid/normal checks pass.
- Exactly **4 cells** fail `cellDeterminant >= 0.001` (`min = 8.334e-5`) and **2 faces** fail `weights >= 0.05` (`min = 0.04622`).
- **Location:** The failing elements are located at $(x, y, z) = (-0.00202194, 0.03483206, 0.01510559)\text{ m}$ and the symmetric opposite inlet $(+0.00202194, -0.03483206, 0.01510559)\text{ m}$, at radial distance $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = 0.0348907\text{ m} \approx R_1 = 0.035\text{ m}$.
- **Correction of Earlier Hypothesis:** These sliver elements are **not** at the $1.4\text{ mm}$ overflow bore ($r = 0.0007\text{ m}$); the $0.35\text{ mm}$ Gmsh size field around the $1.4\text{ mm}$ bore resolves the overflow pipe cleanly.

### 1.2 Geometric Root Cause: Zero-Angle Tangency Cusp
In the baseline CT-35 head (`baselines/ct35/genome.json`), Inlet 1 is a rectangular prism with outer wall at $y = +R_1 = +35.0\text{ mm}$ extending along $x \in [-60, 0]\text{ mm}$, unioned with the cylindrical swirl chamber $x^2 + y^2 \le R_1^2$.
- Along the line $(x, y) \to (0^-, +R_1)$, the cylinder boundary is:
  $$y_{\text{cyl}}(x) = \sqrt{R_1^2 - x^2} \approx R_1 - \frac{x^2}{2 R_1}$$
- At $x = -2.02\text{ mm}$, the radial gap between the flat outer duct wall ($y = 35.0\text{ mm}$) and the cylinder wall ($y = 34.9416\text{ mm}$) is only $\Delta y = 58.4\,\mu\text{m}$, approaching zero tangentially with $\frac{dy}{dx} \to 0$.
- Any conforming unstructured tetrahedral mesher forced to place elements inside the wedge $y \in [\sqrt{R_1^2 - x^2}, R_1]$ as $x \to 0$ must create high-aspect-ratio sliver tetrahedra with near-coplanar face normals, causing the OpenFOAM cell-determinant check (which measures the determinant of face-normal area vectors) to drop below $10^{-3}$.

### 1.3 Geometry-Preserving Remedies
1. **For Exact Baseline CT-35 (`cad/ct35/build.py` / `gmsh_mesh.py`):**
   - Terminal interfaces at $x = \pm 60\text{ mm}$ and the nominal baseline parameters in `baselines/ct35/genome.json` are frozen.
   - In `gmsh_mesh.py`, the cusp wedge at $(x, y) \approx (\mp 2\text{ mm}, \pm 35\text{ mm}), z \in [5.86, 20.0]\text{ mm}$ requires either an explicit local anisotropic/isotropic size-field box (`VIn = 0.25 mm` over $x \in [-6, 6]\text{ mm}, |y| \in [33, 35]\text{ mm}, z \in [5, 21]\text{ mm}$) combined with Gmsh's `Mesh.OptimizeNetgen = 1` + `Relocate2D/3D`, or a prism/hex-core cut that merges the thin cusp wedge into a single polyhedral cell layer.
2. **For Evolved Removable Heads (`cad/program/exotic_graph.py`):**
   - In `ct35-exotic-head-v2`, the `inlet_ramp` layer keeps the terminal port face at $x = \pm 60\text{ mm}$ 100% frozen (exact area $120.264094\text{ mm}^2$, $7.754486\text{ mm} \times 15.508971\text{ mm}$, centroid, and $\pm\hat{x}$ normal), while lofting inward to a throat width $W_{\text{throat}} = \alpha W_0$ ($\alpha \in [0.78, 0.95]$) and/or downward axial drop $\Delta z \in [3, 8]\text{ mm}$ at the cylinder entry.
   - Because the internal ramp intersects the $R_1 = 35\text{ mm}$ cylinder with a modified internal throat geometry while keeping external ports fixed, its hydraulic and separation effects are treated as testable geometric hypotheses pending qualified F2/F4 verification.
