# V2 requirements and decision policy

V1 requirements, preregistration, observations and scientific STATUS retain their original bytes and identities. The v2 TOML is a new **sandbox-only draft**. Its four objectives are represented by registered estimands for printed mass, common-demand reserve, common-flow treated capacity and mechanical energy per treated volume, with oil quality a separate mandatory gate. Their numerical evaluators and physical evidence are later packets; this policy does not implement or qualify them.

`RequirementValue<T>` separates Admitted, Proposed and Unresolved. Admitted is a source claim that must resolve through the existing evidence Registry to exact authenticated, reviewed bytes and the same requirement/applicability scope. Changing the enum string alone does not admit a value. Proposed values and missing inputs remain blockers. The application authenticates reviewers at the existing Registry boundary; incoming documents cannot appoint their own authority. No value or uncertainty is manufactured from a timeout.

The draft names every absent pressure, treated-volume energy, absolute-pressure, continuous-duty and installation-load limit. The historical PDR screen constraint survives only as a cited conflict alternative; it is absent from executable qualified policy. Draft printed passages and nominal machined overflow remain distinct. Neither the analytic fixed load nor a nominal bore proves an allowable, printability or fit.

The seven conflict records retain split, DSD, concentration basis, radial envelope, overflow bore, printed-versus-machined features and PDR authority. D1–D4 numerical values are copied from the exact frozen preregistration, including medians 22, 22, 14.5 and 20 µm. Weighting basis comes separately from the source-bound RS oil-volume convention. A declared scenario cannot silently replace either its values or its number/volume basis. Historical deviations are retained in the original dossiers; copying the preregistered inputs does not retroactively change those runs.

Each missing installation, process, fluid, manufacturing, material, validation, lab, comparator or license input names an accountable role and affected claims. License/disclosure blocks protected export; lab access blocks physical/commercial confirmation. Missing pressure and validation block computational comparator claims as well. These are Unresolved dependencies, not infeasible designs. Benign explicitly synthetic sandbox software can continue through `CampaignRequest::preflight`; the qualified template refuses dispatch.

`DecisionProfile::validate` checks exact reviewed requirement bytes; subject, baseline and applicability bindings; the complete Cartesian product of required gates and scenarios; typed estimand definitions and their expression/population references; numerical and model discrepancy bounds for every estimand; units; approved sampling/joint-model records; selection, stopping, holdout and operating-population policy; and executable fixture references. A missing cold-duty row, empty uncertainty inventory, stale/revoked source, changed fixed candidate, or unbounded discrepancy refuses validation. A finite envelope cannot stand in for an IID tail distribution.

The one-third precision fraction is a run-design budget. It is separate from the confidence procedure and never supplies coverage. The implementation supports declared conservative Bonferroni allocations for simultaneous intervals or adaptive winner qualification, and distinguishes a predeclared fixed-candidate intersection-union conjunction. Marginal bounds at family alpha can support the latter under its registered assumptions; they cannot be relabelled simultaneous coverage. Attempt allocation uses the approved maximum attempt count, and a continuation preserves the experiment-family/policy identity and increments the attempt. Actual globally durable spending/fencing belongs to V5-06; this contract does not claim that storage already exists.

`qualified_admission` means readiness of this policy for **evidence review**. It still requires current source records for limits, external inputs and conflict resolutions. Passing it is not a physical verdict or permission to run a claim-bearing campaign: the trusted observable gate closure, numerical verification, model/physical validation and runtime admission are additional mandatory boundaries. The actual v2 draft remains unresolved, so it cannot pass even this policy-level readiness.

The JSON Schema describes wire shape. The Rust strict decoder additionally rejects duplicate keys, nonfinite input and unknown fields; semantic validators reject negative zero where bounds require a unique representation, unit/source/scope drift and inconsistent inference policy. New crates in V5-03 will supply dimensional expression evaluation without creating a second canonical identity implementation.

Synthetic tests use explicitly authenticated test reviewer events to exercise source closure. They prove software policy behavior, not the truth of those invented measurements or any physical requirement. TR-16, EV-09 and EV-12 deferrals remain active. No numerical physical campaign, GPU work, licensed data acquisition, purchase, contact, license selection or scientific status promotion is part of this change.
