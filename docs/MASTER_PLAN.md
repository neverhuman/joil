## 1. Objective and decision

Create a reproducible design-and-test system for removable deoiling hydrocyclone heads. Represent CT-35 exactly at baseline and allow substantially different inlet paths, wall contours and connectivity later. Identify designs that increase **compliant treated-water capacity** under the same feed, pressure budget, reject policy, footprint and manufacturing condition. A winning computation must survive numerical refinement, model challenges, measured geometry and paired experiments.

The highest-value advance is a reliable evaluator with an expressive design language. More genes, expensive solvers and attractive images cannot substitute for either. Use conservative resource allocation until a complete baseline timing is measured.

Non-goals: altering jarambit; designing an entire pressure vessel; DNS of every micrometre droplet; automatic manufacturing authorization; universal sour-service claims; optimizing grams as the principal objective; predicting fouling life from wall shear; adopting GPU CFD without an end-to-end benchmark; calling an old public reconstruction the best commercial comparator.

Assumptions: initial service is isothermal, flooded, dilute oil in water, rigid walls and negligible free gas/solids. All fluid values, provisional envelope limits and roughness are assumptions until measured. The printed part is an insert in independent pressure-rated containment. The actual vessel drawing, differential pressure history, fluid chemistry, AM process/material data and laboratory access are external dependencies. Missing measurements restrict the claim; they do not prevent benign software development.

Completion has four distinct levels:

1. **Foundation:** reproducible source review, baseline contract, core arithmetic regression tests, exact CAD audit and UI build. No physics validation.
2. **Numerical research release:** full-liner water and droplet pipeline with force, conservation, grid/time/parcel and model-envelope gates. Capable of qualified computational comparisons within its measured domain.
3. **Validated design engine:** independent baseline/conventional-variant experiments and held-out operating conditions support the ranking fidelity. Search algorithm comparisons use equal budgets and independent runs.
4. **Industrial candidate:** matched contemporary commercial comparator, measured as-built parts, statistically supported capacity gain and no required-band, durability, cleanability or qualification regression. Operator and responsible engineers approve application-specific deployment.

Stop the affected lane on changed fixed interfaces, no feasible baseline duty, non-closing oil inventory, ambiguous pressure units, missing provenance, inconsistent force physics, validation extrapolation, unresolved uncertainty larger than the intended improvement, disk/memory pressure, or conflicts with other host work. Preserve diagnostics and continue independent lanes. Do not change limits to manufacture a PASS.

## 4. Representation: express new designs without losing baseline identity

See `GENOME.md` for code-shaped contracts. Implement a versioned **design program**, not one fixed vector whose indexes silently change. Separate four identities: genotype, nominal fluid/solid phenotype, manufacturing variant, and evaluation subject. Retain exact baseline mode with identity round trip. Search can begin with a small smooth head parameterization, then activate channel-graph and implicit-wall families after their own CAD/mesh/physics gates.

Fixed interfaces are immutable geometry, not optimizer genes. The first research lane freezes terminal feed faces and their extensions, oil terminal, the last 0.5 D2 collar, and the entire downstream liner. Interior branches may split, curve, rejoin and change section; both terminal feeds retain their prescribed sense. Keep a separate installed-interface manifest for real hardware. Approximate dimensions cannot certify fit.

Compile `DesignProgram -> GeometryIR -> BRep + labelled surface + solid -> F0Report`. GeometryIR uses SI internally, explicit transforms, ordered primitives and semantic face IDs. No mesh-to-BRep conversion may quietly become a different design. SDF/level-set shapes require bounded geometric approximation plus independent solid/surface checks; if reliable B-rep reconstruction fails, retain an explicit mesh subject and restrict manufacturing/analysis claims. No false STEP file made from an unsewn shell.

Mutations: smooth control-point move, bend/twist of a channel, section-area redistribution, branch split/merge, feature insertion/removal, low-frequency wall-field mode, and build-orientation change. Freeze terminal positions, tangents, areas and collar; reject self-intersections, blind pockets and unremovable supports. Record original, repaired proposal and repair magnitude; large repair is a separate operator. Never silently clamp non-finite values into a valid baseline. Canonicalize graph IDs/order and inactive genes; preserve feature identities across crossover and store lineage/operator version.

Exploration descriptors should capture mechanism: inlet-path topology/branch count, azimuthal wrap, axial injection angle, hydraulic diameter/curvature distribution and chamber asymmetry. Keep expensive flow descriptors in a separate archive. Freeze scaling/binning or CVT centroids before an experiment. Novelty buys evaluation opportunities; it never overrides oil quality or validation gates. Retain topologically different candidates even when their cheap score is uncertain.

## 5. Physics and fidelity

Reference stack: one exact tested OpenFOAM Foundation 14 revision, double precision, CPU/MPI. Pin source, compiler, MPI ABI, dependencies, model source and image digest. Its documented modular Lagrangian framework still needs oil/water-specific integration; available force classes are not proof of correct liquid-drop physics. An environment capability card must report what actually ran. If 14 integration fails, evaluate a separately named alternative; never mix release families or call it the same evaluator.

F0 geometry/manufacturing checks; F1 cheap water-flow initializer or calibrated surrogate; F2 transient Reynolds-stress carrier (SSG, LRR challenge) with qualified size-resolved light-droplet transport; F3a selected WALE LES; F3b breakup/coalescence with size-dependent transport or PBM; F4 independent experiments. These are complementary evidence classes. A costly label does not confer accuracy. No VOF attempt to resolve every 5–50 µm dilute oil interface across the metre-scale device. Local interface-resolved studies may calibrate a constriction model.

OpenCFD v2606 now announces GPU offloading. P03 should investigate it as a separate capability/parity lane with the real turbulence/particle workload, since ignoring that current option would be premature. Its exact model coverage, double precision and end-to-end advantage remain unmeasured here; do not treat release-family migration as a binary plugin swap.

Always include the full liner, with boundary-extension sensitivity. A cached downstream mesh is reusable only with verified topology, interface continuity and conservative mapping; no fixed analytical downstream pressure relation unless independently qualified across the search domain. Full 3D, no imposed half-domain symmetry for accepted vortex statistics.

Carrier: verify pipe flow/hydrostatics and momentum/energy flux before swirling cases. Establish inlet profiles, wall treatment and roughness, pressure units, absolute-pressure reference and gravity. Fixed inlet total flow, one water-outlet pressure and an outer reject-pressure controller set the split. Report PDR as an outcome. Do not prescribe all three independently. Controller holds settled outlet settings while final statistics are collected unless a separate controlled-system duty is defined.

Droplets: audit drag, pressure-gradient/material-acceleration convention, added mass, buoyancy, lift sensitivity, anisotropic dispersion and response-time integration. Density ratio near unity makes generic heavy-particle defaults unsafe. Test tracer following, terminal rise, acceleration response and inward migration in prescribed rotation. No extra centrifugal force in stationary coordinates. Track parcel oil mass, cohort, initial/current diameter and first terminal crossing; wall/in-domain oil is inventory, never captured oil. Restart preserves controller, particles, RNG counters and accumulated statistics.

Do not hard-clip all droplets at one Hinze diameter before the separator and call breakup calibrated. Hinze is a stress/exposure diagnostic. Evolve droplets along their path with a mass-conserving, experimentally constrained rate/daughter model when needed. Challenge no-breakup ranking before any broad search: inlet geometry changes dissipation directly. PBM needs converged classes **and velocity groups**; one shared velocity for all sizes can erase separation. Coalescence/local enrichment, wetting/films, free gas, sand, surfactants and temperature extend the validation domain separately.

## 6. Best tests: independent oracles and error budget

`TEST_CATALOG.json` defines planned challenge families, owners, artifacts and pass/stop behavior. Tests compare independent physical/mathematical expectations, not a duplicated implementation or image snapshots. Each fixture records equation/regime, exact expected value or convergence behavior, units, tolerance rationale and negative controls. Report implemented counts separately from planned families.

Numerical verification dimensions: CAD/surface tolerance, three related spatial meshes, timestep, pressure/corrector tolerances, particle substeps/count, transient averaging, size classes/groups, domain extension and mesh family. Vary one axis at a time before combined finalists. Separate stochastic/time uncertainty from systematic mesh/model/input errors. Richardson/GCI only for suitable refinement behavior after sampling error is small; oscillatory/non-asymptotic results remain unresolved. Use manufactured solutions where supported by the selected equations; do not invent an unavailable OpenFOAM feature.

Initial planning thresholds: water closure ≤0.1%, oil closure ≤1% screen and ≤0.2% finalist, unresolved injected oil ≤0.1%; last mesh/time change <2% Δp and <2 percentage points grade capture. These are **project seeds**, not universal accuracy. At Cin=500 mg/L and split=.02, 0.02 capture error means about 10.20 mg/L concentration error. Thus those loose grade tolerances cannot substantiate a small gain near 20–30 mg/L. Tighten each error contribution to a preallocated fraction of the actual decision margin; target combined uncertainty less than one third of the claimed gain. Bound unexited and retained oil adversarially.

RSM dispersion must be well mixed and consistent with Reynolds-stress covariance. LES dispersion includes unresolved energy only. Check positivity/bounding repair counts and turbulent inlet sensitivity. Benchmarked water curves, velocity profiles, concentration, size-resolved capture and outlet DSD test distinct mechanisms; matching only Δp is insufficient.

Adversarial evaluator tests include changing units by 10³, missing split denominator, number-vs-volume DSD weights, capturing wall-hit/lost parcels, changed tail, spoofed subject hash, stale model calibration, a single lucky seed, outlet recrossings, unknown geometry, baseline zero/no feasible duty, disjoint scenario flow maps, nonmonotone capacity, interpolation across failure islands, inflated reject, equal-pressure extrapolation and insufficient sample duration. Unknown is not a negative physical label for surrogate training.

## 7. Multi-objective decision policy

Definitions and formulas live in `SCIENCE.md`; implement once, expose everywhere. Report both feed capacity and compliant treated-water capacity. At fixed feed Q and reject r, treated capacity is Q(1-r). The feasible operating map is the **intersection at a common flow** of required scenarios and the declared continuous duty band. Do not independently maximize scenario flows then take a minimum. Capacity can be nonmonotone: refine every feasible/infeasible transition; an isolated passing point is only sampled capacity.

At screening duty, compare breakup-aware Cout at matched Δp and split. The square-root constant-Euler flow rescale is an initializer, followed by actual pressure-bracketed CFD; a 2% proximity shortcut is insufficient for a small-gain claim. Handle zero/below-detection Cout with censored observations rather than log infinities.

Use constrained Pareto ranking: maximize conservative common treated capacity, minimize hydraulic energy per compliant treated volume, and minimize sensitivity/regret over measured feed/manufacturing scenarios. Qualify robustness distribution before calling anything p05/CVaR/reliability. If only a finite envelope is known, use worst-case envelope and say so. Manufacturing cost/mass are secondary reported objectives or tie breakers; novelty remains outside industrial fitness. Avoid redundant objectives such as Δp and energy at an identical fixed split unless their definitions differ meaningfully.

Constraints: water quality at research 30 mg/L and separate 20 mg/L stretch, pressure/split/control reachability, geometry/interface, manufacturability/cleanability, operating band, numerical validity and structural/material eligibility. PDR and AM cutoffs remain draft assumptions pending measured envelopes. Feasible dominates physically infeasible; unknown/numerical failures occupy a separate queue and cannot become elite. Never use a weighted sum that buys a constraint violation with another benefit.

Freeze objectives, normalization, reference point, feed scenarios, budgets and uncertainty policy per campaign. Compare random/LHS, competent conventional parametric search, constrained NSGA-II and QD on the **same representation and qualified evaluator**. Use a pilot to set budget and independent-run count; 10 run seeds is an initial main-study target, not a statistical guarantee. Count failures, retries, surrogate data/training and all higher-fidelity audits. Reserve ~10% exploration to audit low-ranked/rejected candidates. Stop cheap-tier ranking when held-out rank reversals or uncertainty invalidate it.

Promotion hypothesis H10: paired capacity point gain ≥10%, paired 95% lower confidence bound >0, same required duty band, no adverse constraint regression. A claim of **at least 10%** requires the lower bound itself >10%. Predeclare one-sided vs two-sided interval, resampling units, endpoint fitting and multiplicity. Physical preparations/builds are independent units; repeated frames or droplets are not independent experimental replicates. Three preparations are a pilot minimum for estimating variance and power, not sufficient evidence by decree.

## 8. Experiments and industry credibility

Create three comparator classes: reproducible CT-35 reconstruction; competent optimized conventional head with equal manufacturing/finish; contemporary installed/commercial liner in the actual service. The last class may require private drawings/data. Public baseline progress is scientifically useful but does not establish commercial superiority.

Use independent pressure-rated containment, metered water and oil introduced after the main pump, controlled feed preparation and a measured inlet DSD at the branch splitter. Characterize both branches; fix manifold, pressure-tap and sampling locations. Measure all three flows, pressures, temperature, dispersed/dissolved oil, DSD, fluid properties and oil inventory. Document analyzer calibration against the chosen laboratory method, detection limits and sampling bias. Fluorescence calibration is oil-specific. DSD instruments and sample lines may alter droplets; qualify them.

Randomized A/B/A blocks, blinded design IDs, independent fluid preparations and repeated builds. Sample all streams with synchronized inventory accounting. Prefer single pass; if recirculating, demonstrate identical reconditioned feed. Separate model calibration, validation/holdout and final blind tests by fluid preparation and geometry family to prevent leakage. Choose final replicates by power/variance pilot. Freeze rig data before fitting kernels; never tune each candidate independently.

Use OSPAR and US offshore values as **reference contexts**, not a site permit. OSPAR 30 mg/L is a flow-weighted monthly performance standard with its prescribed measurement basis; US oil-and-grease rules use different averages/analytes. Obtain site-specific quality metric and discharge/reinjection requirements. Do not claim those regulatory methods are interchangeable with simulated dispersed oil. Use ASME V&V 20 methodology for variable/validation-point error accounting without claiming standards certification. ISO 15156/NACE and AM material acceptance require the applicable licensed edition and process/product-form evidence; no automatic Grade-5 or LPBF sour-service eligibility.

## 9. CAD, FEA and engineering views

CAD gates: one valid connected solid/fluid subject, outward oriented watertight surfaces, no self-intersections/slivers, matched terminal areas/normals, exact collar/tail masks, independent volumes, STEP reimport, millimetre/metre round trip, controlled tessellation and minimum-wall/passage checks. Topological closure alone does not establish printability. Sampled voxel flood fill is a useful screen, not proof against sub-voxel trapped pockets. Refine near minimum features and validate analytical fixtures.

AM gates: printer/material/process-specific overhang and span, powder evacuation through an actual removal path, accessible supports/finishing, drill/ream reject-orifice allowance, cleanability, inspection, tolerance and residual-stress/distortion evidence. Keep build orientation and postprocessing in manufacturing identity, not hidden solver knobs. Ra is not equal to sand-grain roughness. The proposed 45 mm radial envelope conflicts with baseline inlet bosses; wait for a real fit drawing or a versioned port keep-in contract. Do not silently exempt it and call fit verified.

FEA uses solid 3D elements with pressure fields transferred conservatively from a declared CFD timestep/statistic to exact solid faces. Verify resultant force and moment; CFD gauge/absolute/external-pressure distinction controls the actual wall load. Begin with linear elastic static loads from measured differential pressure, seals/contact/supports, handling and thermal mismatch. Add modal/pressure-cycle fatigue, buckling/contact/nonlinearity as warranted. Verify thick-cylinder/Lamé, patch/rigid-body, reaction and energy tests, mesh convergence and cross-solver comparisons (CalculiX reference, optional dolfinx/Code_Aster). No knife plate allowables or fatigue factors transfer. Singular corner peaks need predeclared finite observables. Printed material state/build direction/finish and corrosion reduce confidence until measured.

Two-way FSI only if one-way deformations alter hydraulic clearances beyond their allocated uncertainty or a flow/modal instability matters. Fatigue and erosion predictions remain conditional on spectra and calibrated material/impact data. Pressure testing belongs in independent fixtures, with the responsible engineer defining protocol and limits.

Generate view bundle per exact subject: dimensioned axial/transverse section, exploded head/liner fit, cutaway, wall/passages/overhang map, topology and powder routes, mesh/y+ diagnostics, velocity/pressure/vorticity/Q-criterion slices, oil residence trajectories by size, grade curves, DSD transfer, uncertainty and mesh-convergence plates, operating map and Pareto front, structural displacement/stress/modal shapes, and common-camera baseline/candidate comparison. A field view names source run, mesh, extraction and averaging; governing scalar and plotted field cannot come from different runs without disclosure.

Export separate `solid.glb`, `fluid.glb`, patch meshes, field surfaces and selected curves/trajectories plus `scene.json` with units, coordinate transform, semantic materials, scalar range/legend, cameras, true/deformed scale, clipping and SHA-256. Blender import preserves science data; it must not smooth away certified features or add synthetic flow. Cosmetic views and simulated fields get different labels. No performance-colored geometry until corresponding evidence exists.

## 11. Compute, timeline and approvals needed later

The inspected fleet has **64 physical cores / 128 threads per host**, not 128 physical cores. Memory: xbabe1 ~64 GiB, xbabe2/3 ~128 GiB. GPUs: 4090 24 GiB on xbabe1, 3090 24 GiB on xbabe2/3. Current use and free disk make these shared resources, not a 192-core reservation. Full private inventory has observation timestamp. Use CPU reference first; GPUs suit surrogate training/rendering initially. GPU pressure algebra adoption requires physical parity and ≥1.3× measured complete-workflow speedup/cost benefit; adapter and transfer overhead count.

Pre-pilot **planning envelopes**, not measured predictions: F2 100–10,000+ core-hours per operating point; F3 1,000–100,000+ core-hours. Two pressure-matched F2 solves can cost 200–20,000+ core-hours per design before robustness/refinement; a 7-flow map 700–70,000+. Ten designs with two 100-core-hour solves need 2,000 core-hours: ~62.5 h on an actually allocated 32 cores or 31.25 h on 64, before overhead; the high planning value is 100× longer, and is not a proven upper bound. Full numerical qualification multiplies cost further. Replace these wide envelopes after the first real carrier pilot; do not promise hundreds of designs overnight.

Cost stress test from the actual baseline: 2% of 4 m³/h through a 1.4 mm bore implies ~14.4 m/s mean bore speed. A flow-aligned cell at 32 cells across the bore and CFL 0.5 suggests ~1.5 µs, so even ten V/Q spans ~6.6 million steps. Neither that timestep nor the required averaging duration is measured; this arithmetic explains why optimistic minutes-per-design promises are excluded. If pilot cost prevents sufficient qualified comparisons, stop scaling and invest in verified model reduction/active experimental design. Do not simply reduce physical/statistical duration.

Measured ETA model: `mesh + simulated_duration / dt * seconds_per_step + controller + particles + extraction + queue/retries`. Core-hours include allocated ranks for all phases, including failures. Use observed p50/p90/p95 step and mesh times; predeclare physical/statistical stop criteria. Provisional budgets: 5 GiB bootstrap, baseline pilot ≤20 GiB only after a lease, selectively retained full fields, no auto-expanding LES outputs. Host3 free disk was only ~82 GiB at inspection; avoid heavy fields there until space is deliberately allocated. No cross-node MPI until network/ABI tests pass; independent cases can be distributed without a fast interconnect.

Calendar estimates assume a dedicated qualified engineer per active role and usable rig access: foundation now; water-hydraulics prototype about 2–4 weeks; qualified dilute-oil computational engine about 5–10 weeks; initial evidence-driven search about 8–14 weeks **after sufficient validation data**; manufactured industrial demonstration roughly 3–6+ months, dominated by rig, print, comparator and qualification lead time. These are work/lead-time envelopes with dependencies, not commitments or measured software throughput. A single implementer takes longer. Reestimate after P03/P04 and laboratory readiness.

No sudo needed for the bootstrap. OpenFOAM/MPI/CalculiX binaries were not on the probed host PATH; shell environments/images may still contain them. `COMPUTE.md` gives inspection-first installation steps. Ask for administrator action only when an isolated path cannot satisfy the tested environment. Do not change shared drivers, CUDA or MPI globally.

## 12. Receipts, status and final handoff

Every packet emits `docs/receipts/<packet>-<UTC>.json` (small public-safe summary) plus immutable detailed run artifacts in ignored storage. Required fields: packet/owner, input HEAD, modified paths, input/evaluator/requirement identities, commands with cwd/env/exit/time, artifact sizes/SHA-256, planned vs executed tests, science eligibility, failures, allocation and next action. Public receipts omit installation/customer/raw private input details; private evidence indexes retain them. No receipt claims physical validation from a software unit test.

Use concise updates on findings, uncertainty and the next resolving step. Mark a packet complete only when its artifacts and gate evidence exist. UI reads derived status; a worker cannot elevate itself to validated. Integrator resolves concurrent work through exact commits and scoped diffs. Final handoff must be usable by a fresh worker: read-first list, current HEAD, exact working command, environment lock, data/receipts, scope of confidence, open dependencies, rollback and next packet. Do not start long-running background work or future monitoring without a separate user request.
