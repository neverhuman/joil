# V5 physical confirmation

**State: unsigned draft; not registered, approved, or executed.** No fabrication, purchase, lab booking, or external messaging is authorized by this planning work.

The complete draft protocol is [protocol.json](../../experiments/rig/v5/protocol.json). It requires a separately engineered pressure-rated containment assembly, pre-test proof and independent review by the pressure, materials/manufacturing, process, and experimental leads. The comparator identity and same-duty policy must be frozen before preregistration. CT-35 is a research comparator unless provenance and applicability are reviewed.

Power planning uses the paired percent-effect variability from at least three independent pilot build/preparation pairs. The sample-size helper reports a conservative normal approximation, rounds up, and requires statistician review. Confirmation units are independently manufactured specimens paired within independently prepared fluid batches. Reprinting or resampling one specimen/batch does not create additional independent units.

Each independent unit uses concealed randomized blinded A1/B/A2 blocks. The draft A1/A2 drift limits preserve protocol v1: treated-oil concentration drift above 10% or pressure drift above 5% invalidates the paired block; preregistration must approve or replace these draft limits before collection. Instrument and sample-line lag, clock synchronization, calibration and recovery, LOQ, all inlet/treated/reject oil concentration and volume-weighted DSD, feed/treated/reject flow, every pressure tap, temperature, and oil/water inventory are recorded. Reduction aligns lag-corrected streams on common windows. Censored or absent samples are not imputed, and no extrapolation is permitted. Independent sample and common systematic uncertainties are retained separately and propagated through synchronized concentration-flow mass integrals. Cross-sensor covariance must be supplied in future admitted analyses rather than silently ignored.

Calibration and holdout are separate. The calibration manifest is empty and intended for measurement/reduction controls. The holdout manifest contains no outcomes and stays sealed until the DecisionProfile, reduction version, required scenarios, and analysis commit are frozen. No calibration subject or preparation may reappear in holdout.

Confirmation analysis pairs independent-unit results under an identical duty, band, pressure policy, and required scenario family. It reports the paired estimate and confidence bound. A `>=10%` claim is allowed only when the lower bound is at least `10%`; otherwise report the supported smaller or negative result. A favorable mean, a single preparation, or any failed required scenario cannot qualify. This software fixture does not perform canonical `DecisionProfile` admission, material qualification, model validation, or industrial installation approval.

Private technical dossiers retain lineage, first-seen time, exact subject/variant, evidence, controls/interventions, and human decisions. Public export is an exact reviewed allowlist and rejects private customer/facility identifiers. Research thresholds and software novelty never authorize industrial installation.
