# Reference registry

Checked 14 October 2026. Links are evidence for their stated scope only. No raw copyrighted papers or standards are redistributed. Proposed project tolerances, ETAs and improvement hypotheses are not source results.

| ID | Primary source | Supported scope and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| R01 | [Colman & Thew, US4576724A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US4576724A/enI) | Patent example/ratios and small axial oil overflow support the research reconstruction. Inlet aspect, bore length, shell and installation interfaces remain choices. OCR ambiguities require original drawing/text verification before an exact experimental reproduction. Not a commercial benchmark or freedom-to-operate opinion. |
| R02 | [Ditria & Rawlins, SPE-213033-MS, 2023](https://eprocess-tech.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/spe-213033-ms.pdf) | Manufacturer-authored industry operating context, PDR/reject and different liner generations; DO15 is 38 mm with a reported 200–700 BPD range. Its minimum pressure refers to inlet-to-reject. Does not certify our CT-35 geometry or promise its performance. |
| R03 | [Maddahian et al., Petroleum Science 9, 2012](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s12182-012-0236-3.pdf) | Two different hydrocyclone geometries and comparisons to Bai velocity/Young separation measurements. Figure 6 is not automatically a droplet-grade curve for our CAD. Original experimental geometry/data and uncertainty must be recovered before V&V use. |
| R04 | [Bram et al., 2020, author institution record](https://vbn.aau.dk/en/publications/control-oriented-modeling-and-experimental-validation-of-a-deoili/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/pr8091010) | Control-oriented model and measured oil-in-water instrumentation. Institution record verified; publisher page initially blocked. Recover full article through institution open-access copy; do not mark every geometry ratio independently verified from metadata. |
| R05 | [Husveg et al., MIC 41(1), 2020](https://www.mic-journal.no/PDF/2020/MIC-2020-1-2.pdf) | Once-through oil/water experimental arrangement, DSD/coalescing pump and PDR control context. Different fluids/hardware; useful rig design evidence, not our validation data. |
| R06 | [OSPAR produced-water response assessment](https://oap.ospar.org/en/ospar-assessments/quality-status-reports/qsr-2023/thematic-assessments/offshore-industry/response/) · [Commission discharges page](https://www.ospar.org/work-areas/oic/discharges) | Dispersed-oil 30 mg/L flow-weighted monthly context and reference-method distinction. No inference that a research CFD point establishes compliance or that this is a site permit. |
| R07 | [Official eCFR, 40 CFR 435.13](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-N/part-435/subpart-A/section-435.13) | Offshore-subcategory oil-and-grease 42 mg/L daily and 29 mg/L 30-day values, subject to rule applicability. Analyte/method/averages differ from our snapshot dispersed-oil metric. |
| R08 | [ASME V&V 20 catalogue](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/standard-for-verification-and-validation-in-computational-fluid-dynamics-and-heat-transfer) | Accuracy assessment is variable- and validation-point-specific with experiment and solution uncertainty. Catalogue reviewed; licensed full text/edition required for formal standards claims. |
| R09 | [NASA: examining spatial convergence](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/wind/valid/tutorial/spatconv.html) | Systematic refinement and convergence-estimation methodology. Does not set a universal sufficient cell count or accuracy threshold for oil separation. |
| R10 | [Foundation OpenFOAM 14 release](https://openfoam.org/release/14/) | Release and modular solver/Lagrangian capabilities. Source and exact model integration must be pinned and tested; released infrastructure is not a turnkey validated oil-drop model. |
| R11 | [Foundation modular Lagrangian guide](https://doc.cfd.direct/openfoam/lagrangian/) | Architecture/model configuration reference. Freeze compatible source/docs; inspect force conventions and validate against light-drop elementary cases. |
| R12 | [Foundation Ubuntu installation](https://openfoam.org/download/14-ubuntu/) | Official package/repository procedure for supported Ubuntu releases. Verify signatures/version and record digest; do not mix OpenCFD packages. |
| R13 | [OpenCFD v2606 infrastructure announcement](https://www.openfoam.com/news/main-news/openfoam-v2606/infrastructure) | Official search-indexed announcement of GPU offloading, checked; full page fetch returned 403. Exact supported models, precision and our oil workflow remain a capability/benchmark task. |
| R14 | [PETSc GPU support roadmap](https://petsc.org/release/overview/gpu_roadmap/) | GPU algebra support; not proof that Foundation CFD/particles run on GPU or that transfer/assembly costs pay off. |
| R15 | [ISO 15156-3:2020 catalogue](https://www.iso.org/cms/live/live/en/sites/isoorg/contents/data/standard/07/96/79660.html?browse=tc) | Standard scope for alloys in H2S-containing production environments. Full applicable tables/edition/operator rules not audited; no specific alloy or AM sour-service qualification claimed. |

Outstanding literature acquisition: original Young 1994 and Bai 2009 apparatus/data, uncertainty and diameter definitions; original Colman–Thew dimensions rather than OCR alone; liquid/liquid breakup/coalescence and anisotropic well-mixed dispersion closure data in the actual regime; current commercial comparator performance under identical feeds. These are P03/P05/P06 dependencies, never fabricated or inferred from a shared nominal diameter.
